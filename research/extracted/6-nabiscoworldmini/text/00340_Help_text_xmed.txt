SELECTING A HOLE TO PLAY:There are 4 ways to play this game:1. Practice a single hole over and over2. Practice the Front 9 holes in order3. Practice the Back 9 holes in order4. Play our 18 Hole Miniature Golf ContestIn the interest of saving download time, the Holes are loaded from our server as needed.  To practice a single hole, from the Main Menu simply watch for that hole to finish loading and click on its icon.  To play the Front 9, Back 9, or 18 Hole Contest, click on one of the larger icons near the top of the screen.THE OBJECT OF THE GAME:Try to putt your ball into the hole in the fewest number of strokes.  Avoid the obstacles by carefully timing your shot.The maximum number of strokes that can be taken on any hole is 14.TEEING OFF:When on the tee, move the mouse left and right or up and down, then click to choose the desired tee position.PUTTING THE BALL:Use the mouse arrow to help you aim the ball.  Near the hole you'll have to use the angle of the putter as the arrow disappears.  Click and hold down the mouse button to pull back the putter.  The longer you hold down the button, the harder you will hit the ball.  Release the button to strike the ball.If you have already drawn your club back and are unhappy with the power youhave selected, simply place the cursor on your ball and release the mousebutton.  This will reset the selected shot.WHO GOES NEXT?:In a multi-player game, players take turns (first player 1, then player 2) hitting the ball.  However, if a player putts and comes up just short of the hole, the game will allow him or her to "putt out" to complete the hole and move the ball out of the other players' way.  Pay close attention to your ball color and player number to make sure it's your turn.
